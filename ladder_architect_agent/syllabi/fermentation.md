# Fermentation Ladder Template

Six tiers:

- Tier 0: Culture
  - Buy a SCOBY or starter from a person, not a website.
  - Evidence: photo with the person + one thing they wish they’d known.

- Tier 1: First batch
  - Kombucha first ferment.
  - Evidence: pH under 3.5 + photo of pellicle.
  - Twist: blind taste vs store bottle, judged by someone at home.
  - Unlocks four tracks: Bottle, Grain, Koji, Orchard.

- Tier 2: Tracks (pick one)
  - Bottle: second ferment, carbonation, flavouring.
  - Grain: ginger beer bug from scratch.
  - Koji: buy spores, make shio koji, cook with it for someone.
  - Orchard: cider from pressed local apples.

- Tier 3: World nodes
  - Tour a brewery/cidery or visit a miso/koji maker.
  - Or bring a bottle to a homebrew club meeting for judging.
  - Evidence: stamp, photo, or scoresheet.

- Tier 4: Long ones
  - Miso (six months minimum).
  - Sake (koji, moto, multi-stage).
  - Hard kombucha or hard ginger beer (yeast selection, ABV with hydrometer).

- Tier 5: Give it away
  - Serve a full ferment flight to six people who aren’t your household.
  - Or teach a Tier 0 node to someone and stamp their passport.

Pilgrimage capstones (one per track):
- Koji → Japan (Kyoto miso houses, Niigata sake brewery).
- Orchard → Normandy or Somerset.
- Bottle → Belgian lambic country.
- Domestic “pilgrimage lite”: Asheville fermentation scene.
